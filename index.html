ABOUT THEIR JOURNEY
Rohit Sharma's cricketing journey, from humble beginnings to becoming a global icon, is marked by remarkable talent, dedication, and a series of accolades. Born in Nagpur, Maharashtra, he rose through the ranks, showcasing his potential in domestic cricket before making his debut for India in 2007. He's known for his aggressive batting style, particularly in the limited-overs formats, and has accumulated numerous records, including the most sixes in international cricket and the most double centuries in ODIs. Beyond his batting prowess, Sharma has also led the Mumbai Indians to five IPL titles and has served as captain of the Indian national team in various formats.
